Interstate Migration in Australia

[Introduction] Internal migration across Australia refers to the movement of people between states and territories, which affects how the population is distributed throughout the country. This project uses Net Interstate Migration (NIM) data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics to analyse the difference between the number of people moving into and out of each state or territory by age, gender, and financial year.
